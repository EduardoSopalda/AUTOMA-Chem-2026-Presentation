# Sources

Every company figure on screen is listed here, quoted exactly, with where it came from.

## dsm-firmenich Integrated Annual Report 2025

Page: [Climate change](https://annualreport.dsm-firmenich.com/2025/sustainability-statements/environmental-information/climate-change.html). Table: "Greenhouse gas emissions Scope 1, 2 & 3". Unit: 1,000 tonnes CO2e. Checked 25 September 2026.

| Line in the report | 2025 |
|---|---|
| Gross Scope 1 CO2e emission | 581.7 |
| Total Scope 2 CO2e emissions, market based | 140.0 |
| Total Scope 2 CO2e emissions, location based | 464.3 |
| Scope 3 CO2e emissions | 10,280.1 |
| Total CO2e emissions, market based | 11,001.8 |
| Total CO2e emissions, location based | 11,326.1 |

The report's own home page gives the total rounded as "11,002 kt CO2e" (Greenhouse gas emissions Scope 1, 2 and 3). The same page states Scope 1 and 2 market based as 722 kt (582 + 140).

## What goes on screen in Act 5

| On screen | Where it comes from | Status |
|---|---|---|
| 11,002 kt CO2e | Total, market based, 11,001.8, rounded as on the report's home page | Verified |

| 140 kt | Scope 2 market based, 140.0 | Verified |
| 464 kt | Scope 2 location based, 464.3. Found in the report's own table, so the third party source is no longer needed. | Verified |

Note: the total itself also depends on the method: 11,002 kt market based, 11,326 kt location based. This isn't used on screen, but it's worth knowing if someone asks.
