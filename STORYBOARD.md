# Storyboard: From Brasília to the Plant Floor

Phase 2 draft, 25 September 2026. For Edu to review.

This follows the approved acts in CLAUDE.md sections 5, 5b and 5c. Nothing new has been added. The work here is grouping beats into clicks, putting them in order and setting time budgets.

**Timings are budgets, not measurements.** They assume 140 words a minute until your phone recording gives us your real pace. When the cut script arrives, I'll put each spoken line against its click and replace the budgets with real numbers.

## How to read this

* **#** is the click number. A click advances one thought, and everything in one row happens after that one click, in order.
* **Cue** is the words you're saying when you press. They come from the current script and may change when you cut it.
* **Chain** means one animation leading straight into the next within the same click. Nothing moves on to the next beat by itself.
* **Hold** means a protected silence (section 5b): nothing moves at all.
* **Archetype** refers to the brand bible, page 12: Statement, Drawing, Contrast, Evidence, Bridge, Question.
* **Always on screen:** the paper, the figure (from Act 1 on), the living title block at the bottom right, and the ghost dimension line at about 12% ink (from the end of Act 1 until it gets its label in Act 9).

## Where I disagree with the brief

**The click count.** Section 5b sets 28 to 34 clicks. This storyboard has 43. I got there by merging every beat I could without timing a visual to a sentence that comes later. Getting to 34 would mean nine more merges. Each one chains an animation across sentences on a timer, which means the drawing sets your pace instead of your voice. That breaks section 7: "Edu sets the pace with his voice."

My advice: accept about 43. That's one click every 14 seconds, calm for a clicker. If you want 34, these are the nine merges I'd make, most harmless first:

1. 10 into 9 (sight lines chained after the RACI laugh)
2. 37 into 38 (the fade and "Humans do. Always." as one click)
3. 22 into 21 (callouts chained after the Scope 2 split)
4. 7 into 8 (the dots arrive and leave on one click)
5. 31 into 32 (cracks, then the Congresso reveal)
6. 13 into 14 (desire paths, then "Complete. Used.")
7. 3 into 4 (lift to plan, then callouts)
8. 29 into 28 (the teleport arc chained after the city)
9. 40 into 41 (photograph, then nursery)

Numbers 5, 6 and 9 would each put a line you care about on a timer. I would not make those three.
